Why this exists

Leaving a business you built feels permanent. There is no going back for a few shifts if you get it wrong, and most owners work it out alone. I did. I would have paid for a confidential sounding board who had been through it and had nothing to sell me.

Playing golf every day sounds great for about a month.

What we can talk about

Structure and pay

Holding companies, retained earnings, corporate investing, and how profit actually reaches you.

Tax and estate

LCGE, AMT, family trusts, deemed disposition, probate, and smoothing tax over a lifetime.

Investing basics

Index vs. active, fees, asset allocation and location, lump sum vs. averaging in.

Retirement mechanics

Withdrawal rates, sequence-of-returns risk, registered vs. non-registered accounts, insurance.

The other half

Purpose, relationships, legacy, and what a good life looks like when your time is yours.

What this is, and isn't

It is

  • A conversation between two people who have both been owners
  • A way to walk into a professional's office already asking better questions
  • Confidential. Nothing is shared or judged.
  • A handful of emails, and maybe a call if it helps

It isn't

  • Financial, tax, or legal advice. I'm not a CFP, accountant, or lawyer.
  • Product or fund recommendations
  • Help negotiating with your franchisor
  • A discussion of my own finances
  • An on-call service. Replies take a few days.

I will always suggest you talk to at least two independent professionals and compare, not just the one a friend recommended.
