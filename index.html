How this works

This is a private, thoughtful conversation for franchise owners who are starting to think seriously about life after business.

  • This is an informed perspective, not a substitute for professional advice. I'm not a CFP, lawyer, or accountant. Any decisions should be reviewed by a qualified professional.
  • It's not about compensation discussions. I won't be part of those conversations.
  • Numbers stay private. This is a personal and financial check-in, not an audit.
  • Conversations can be anonymous.
  • It's a space to think through your situation, your circumstances, and the questions that matter most.
  • Replies take time.
  • No obligation, no drama.
  • This is not advice — it's an informed second opinion, and a way to think more clearly about the future.
  • The goal is simple: help franchisees ask better questions, understand their options, and make more informed decisions about life after ownership.
